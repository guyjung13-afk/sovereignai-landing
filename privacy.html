Privacy Policy

How SovereignAI LLC collects, uses, and protects information.

SovereignAI LLC ("SovereignAI," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you visit our website at sovereignai.llc or contact us through the channels provided on this site.

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form or email us directly, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, organization, role, and message content.

This website does not use analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising cookies. It is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages; as with any web host, GitHub may process standard server log data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site. All fonts, styles, and scripts are served from this domain; no third-party resources are loaded when you browse the site.

How Inquiries Are Routed

The briefing request form on this site is not a first-party endpoint. Submissions are transmitted to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-relay service, which forwards them to our inbox by email. FormSubmit processes your submission under its own privacy terms. If you prefer that your inquiry not pass through a third-party relay, email us directly at guy@sovereignai.llc.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential engagements, communicate about our services, and improve our website. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Sovereignty Commitment

SovereignAI is built on the principle that organizational data should remain under organizational control. The zero-egress guarantee applies to client deployments, which operate within customer-controlled perimeters under dedicated contractual terms. This public website and its inquiry form are separate from those environments and rely on the third-party services described above.

Data Retention

We retain inquiry and correspondence data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information submitted through this website. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at guy@sovereignai.llc.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to SovereignAI LLC at guy@sovereignai.llc.
